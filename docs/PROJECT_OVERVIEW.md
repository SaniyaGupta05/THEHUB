# THE HUB & HUBKLUB — Project Overview & Feature Guide

---

## 1. What is this Project?

**The HUB** is a premier luxury fashion and boutique retail brand located in Vadodara, Gujarat. 

This project bridges the gap between **physical in-store retail shopping** and **digital customer engagement** through a unified system consisting of two main parts:
1. **The Customer Mobile Application (The HUB & HUBKLUB App)**: A luxury shopping and loyalty membership application for customers on their smartphones.
2. **The Store Billing & Management Dashboard (Staff POS Counter)**: A web portal used by store cashiers and store managers at physical billing counters.

### The Big Idea
Traditionally, when a customer shops at a physical boutique counter, paper bills get lost, loyalty points are forgotten, and store cashiers have no easy way of knowing a customer’s shopping history. 

This project solves that by making the **in-store billing counter** and the **customer's smartphone** talk to each other in real time:
* When a customer makes a purchase at the physical store counter, their bill and reward points instantly show up on their mobile app.
* When a customer walks up to the billing counter, the cashier can immediately see their points and apply them as a cash discount on the spot (where **1 point = ₹1 rupee**).

## 2. What We Provide in the Mobile Application

The mobile app is designed for the customer. It gives them a complete boutique shopping experience, a personal loyalty wallet, and a permanent digital archive of every bill and receipt.

### A. Luxury Shopping Experience
* **Curated Fashion Catalog**: Browse wedding, celebratory, and festive couture collections (Sherwanis, Kurta Sets, Bandhgalas, Tuxedos, Indo-Western, and Accessories).
* **High-Definition Outfits & Zoom**: View garments with fabric descriptions, styling advice, and size selection.
* **Shopping Cart & Simple Checkout**: Add items to bag, apply coupons, redeem loyalty points directly for cash discounts, and enter delivery addresses.
* **Wishlist**: Save favorite looks for upcoming weddings, festivals, or store trials.

### B. HUBKLUB Loyalty Membership Hub
* **Digital Membership Card**: 
  * Displays the customer’s membership tier (**Classic**, **Gold**, or **Platinum**) and unique membership ID.
  * No need to carry physical plastic cards; the phone acts as the digital pass.
* **Membership Eligibility Tracker**:
  * Shows how close a customer is to qualifying for HUBKLUB membership (e.g. ₹1,000 threshold on a single purchase).
  * Eligible customers can tap one button to enroll instantly for free.
* **Live Points Wallet & Balance**:
  * Clearly displays available reward points and their exact rupee value (e.g., **1,700 points = ₹1,700 cash value**).
* **Transparent Earning Rules**:
  * Up to ₹20,000 purchase: **3% back** in points.
  * ₹20,001 to ₹50,000 purchase: **5% back** in points.
  * Above ₹50,000 purchase: **6% back** in points.
  * **20% Annual VIP Milestone Bonus**: Customers who spend over ₹1,00,000 in a year automatically unlock 20% extra bonus points on every purchase.
* **Accurate Points History (Ledger)**:
  * A clear record showing every point earned (from in-store bills or online purchases), every point redeemed, and welcome bonuses.
  * Filterable by Earned, Redeemed, or Expired points.
* **Points Expiry Tracking**:
  * Points are valid for 24 months.
  * Customers can see which batches of points are expiring and when, ensuring they never lose their savings.

### C. Digital Bills & Order History ("My Orders & Store Bills")
* **Paperless Receipts**: Every in-store purchase made at The HUB Vadodara store counter automatically appears here.
* **Store Counter Badge**: In-store bills are clearly marked with a store icon and counter details.
* **Bill Details**: Shows bill date, total amount paid, payment method used, items bought, and points earned.
* **Easy Exchanges**: Customers never have to search for physical paper receipts when visiting the store for alterations or inquiries.

### D. Activity Notifications & Alerts
* **Real-Time Purchase Confirmations**: Instant alert when an in-store bill is generated at the counter.
* **Points Credit Notifications**: Informs the customer of points added to their wallet and their new total balance.
* **Milestone Alerts**: Notifies customers when they unlock VIP tier benefits or annual bonuses.

## 3. What We Provide in the Billing Dashboard (Staff Portal)

The dashboard is designed for **store managers**, **cashiers**, and **back-office staff** working at the physical store in Vadodara.

### A. Counter POS Billing Counter (Shoppers Stop Style)
* **Instant Customer Lookup**:
  * The cashier simply types the customer's mobile number, name, or membership ID.
  * The system immediately displays:
    * Customer Name & Contact info.
    * Membership Tier (Non-Member, Classic, Gold).
    * Available Points Balance and exact Rupee Value (e.g., "1,250 points = ₹1,250 discount available").
    * Spend History & VIP Milestone status.
* **Instant Points Redemption at Counter**:
  * The cashier can ask the customer: *"You have ₹1,250 in HUBKLUB points. Would you like to use them today?"*
  * With one click, points are deducted and the bill total updates automatically.
* **Automatic Points Calculation**:
  * Cashiers do not have to calculate reward percentages manually.
  * The system computes points based on the net bill amount and credits them instantly to the customer's account.
* **Payment Mode Selection**:
  * Supports Cash, Credit/Debit Card, and UPI.
* **Digital Invoice Generation & Tally Sync**:
  * Generates an official invoice number (e.g., `HUB-POS-2026-950F72`) and a Tally sales voucher reference.
  * Syncs the receipt immediately to the customer's mobile app.

### B. In-Store Sales & Invoices Ledger
* A complete, live history of every bill issued at the physical store counter.
* Search and filter by invoice number, customer phone, date, cashier name, or payment mode.
* Instant view of gross revenue, loyalty discounts given, and net collection.

### C. Customer Directory
* Complete list of all registered customers and walk-in shoppers.
* Shows total lifetime spend, annual spend for the current calendar year, tier status, and total active points.

### D. Inventory & Catalog Visibility
* Real-time view of all boutique products, sizes, pricing, and active collections.

## 4. How Mobile App & Billing Dashboard Work Together

Here is a real-life example of how a shopping visit works from start to finish:

```
                  CUSTOMER VISITS STORE
                            │
                            ▼
    1. Customer picks an outfit at THE HUB Vadodara (e.g., ₹10,000)
                            │
                            ▼
    2. Cashier enters customer's mobile number in the Billing Dashboard
                            │
                            ▼
    3. Dashboard immediately pulls customer's profile & points balance
       (e.g., 300 points available = ₹300 discount)
                            │
                            ▼
    4. Customer agrees to redeem 300 points:
       • Bill Amount: ₹10,000
       • Points Redeemed: -₹300
       • Net Payable: ₹9,700
       • Points Earned on this bill: +291 points
                            │
                            ▼
    5. Cashier completes checkout.
       The invoice is stored with Tally voucher sync.
                            │
                            ▼
    6. Customer opens their mobile app on their phone:
       • Notification arrives: "In-Store Bill #HUB-POS-2026-XXXX Completed"
       • "My Orders" displays the full digital store bill
       • Points wallet immediately updates with the new balance
```
