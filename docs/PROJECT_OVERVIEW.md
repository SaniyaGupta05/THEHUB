# THE HUB & HUBKLUB — Project Overview & Feature Guide (Including GO SILVER 2027–2028)

---

## 1. What is this Project?

**The HUB** is a premier luxury fashion and boutique retail brand located at Spentha Complex, Alkapuri, Vadodara, Gujarat.

This project bridges the gap between **physical in-store retail shopping** and **digital customer engagement** through a unified omnichannel system consisting of two main parts:
1. **The Customer Mobile Application (The HUB & HUBKLUB App)**: A luxury shopping, loyalty membership, and **GO SILVER (2027–2028)** celebration tracking application for customers on their smartphones.
2. **The Store Billing & Management Dashboard (Staff POS Counter)**: A fast, modern web portal used by store cashiers and store managers at physical billing counters.

### The Big Idea
Traditionally, when a customer shops at a physical boutique counter, loyalty points are forgotten, and store cashiers have no easy way of knowing a customer’s shopping history or special milestone rewards.

This project solves that by making the **in-store billing counter** and the **customer's smartphone** talk to each other in real time:
* When a customer makes a purchase at the physical store counter, their digital bill, reward points, and **GO SILVER celebration progress** instantly show up on their mobile app.
* When a customer walks up to the billing counter, the cashier can immediately see their points balance, apply points as an instant cash discount (**1 point = ₹1 rupee**), or enroll a walk-in shopper on the spot using **OTP verification**.

## 2. What We Provide in the Customer Mobile Application

The mobile app is designed for the customer (Android & iOS). It gives them a complete boutique shopping experience, a personal loyalty wallet, a live tracker for the **GO SILVER 25th Year Celebration**, and a permanent digital archive of every bill and receipt.

### A. Luxury Shopping Experience
* **Curated Fashion Catalog**: Browse wedding, celebratory, and festive couture collections (Sherwanis, Kurta Sets, Bandhgalas, Tuxedos, Indo-Western, and Accessories).
* **High-Definition Outfits & Zoom**: View garments with fabric descriptions, styling advice, and size selection.
* **Shopping Bag & Fast Checkout**: Add items to bag, apply coupons, redeem loyalty points directly for cash discounts, and enter delivery addresses.
* **Wishlist**: Save favorite looks for upcoming weddings, festivals, or store trials.

### B. HUBKLUB Loyalty Membership ("The Point That Matters")
* **Digital Membership Card**:
  * Displays the customer’s membership tier (**Classic**, **Gold**, or **Platinum**) and unique membership ID.
  * No need to carry physical plastic cards; the smartphone acts as the digital pass.
* **Membership Eligibility Tracker**:
  * Shows how close a customer is to qualifying for HUBKLUB membership (**₹1,000 minimum on a single purchase**).
  * Eligible customers can enroll instantly.
* **Live Points Wallet & Balance**:
  * Clearly displays available reward points and their exact rupee value (**1 Point = ₹1 Rupee**).
* **Transparent Single-Purchase Earning Slabs ("The Point That Matters")**:
  * **Up to ₹20,000** (single invoice): **3% of invoice value** back in points.
  * **₹20,001 to ₹50,000** (single invoice): **5% of invoice value** back in points.
  * **₹50,001 and above** (single invoice): **6% of invoice value** back in points.
  * **20% Annual Milestone Bonus**: Customers who accumulate **₹1,00,000** in spending within a year automatically unlock **20% extra bonus points** on the points earned in that year.
* **Accurate Points History (Ledger) & 2-Year Validity**:
  * A complete itemized record showing every point earned, redeemed, or expired.
  * Points are valid for **24 months (2 years)** from the date of earning, with expiry alerts for upcoming batches.

### C. GO SILVER (2027–2028) — 25th Year Celebration Tracker
* Dedicated silver-themed milestone card inside the app showing the customer's progress toward The HUB's **25th Year Silver Jubilee Celebration (2028)** rewards.
* Tracks accumulated purchases during the celebration window (**1st Jan 2027 to 1st Aug 2028**) and highlights unlocked rewards (Event Pass and Surprise Gifts).

### D. Digital Bills & Order History ("My Orders & Store Bills")
* **Paperless Bills**: Every in-store purchase made at The HUB Vadodara store counter automatically appears in the app.
* **Bill Details**: Shows invoice number, Tally voucher reference, date, gross amount, loyalty discount applied, net paid, payment method, and points earned.

## 4. What We Provide in the Billing Dashboard (Staff POS Portal)

The dashboard is designed for **store cashiers**, **sales staff**, and **store managers** at physical billing counters in Vadodara.

### A. 3-Step Express POS Billing Counter
* **Step 1 — Instant Customer Lookup & Inline OTP Enrollment**:
  * Search any customer by mobile number, name, or ID in under 2 seconds.
  * Immediately displays Customer Name, Tier, Available Points, Rupee Discount Value, and the **GO SILVER (2027–2028)** status strip.
  * **+ New Customer (OTP)**: If a walk-in shopper is not registered, the cashier can enroll them right from the billing screen with mobile OTP verification and optional **+100 Welcome Bonus Points**.
* **Step 2 — Gross Bill Entry & Quick Presets**:
  * Enter the bill amount or tap quick preset buttons (₹1,500 to ₹25,000) and select payment mode (**UPI / Card**, **Cash**, or **Card Swipe**).
* **Step 3 — One-Click HUBKLUB Points Redemption**:
  * Cashier can apply preset point redemptions (₹500, ₹1,00, ₹2,000, or **Redeem Max**) where **1 Point = ₹1 instant discount**.
* **Automatic Single-Invoice Slab Calculation & Digital Invoicing**:
  * Automatically computes the 3%, 5%, or 6% points earned on the net payable amount.
  * Generates digital invoices with **The HUB** branding, GSTIN, Invoice Number, Tally Voucher Reference, Loyalty Statement, and **GO SILVER (2027–2028)** celebration progress synced directly to the customer's app.

### B. Additional Store Management Modules
* **Counter Token Verification**: Validate and confirm reward redemption tokens/QR codes generated from the customer mobile app.
* **Customer Directory & Enrollment**: Browse all enrolled members, view lifetime and annual spend, or enroll new customers.
* **ERP & Tally Integration Health**: Monitor real-time connectivity with Tally ERP and Shopify catalog adapters.
* **Daily Reconciliation Report**: End-of-day audit trail of all store bills, points issued, points redeemed, and net revenue.

## 5. How Mobile App & POS Billing Counter Work Together

```
                  CUSTOMER VISITS THE HUB VADODARA
                             │
                             ▼
     1. Customer selects outfits at Spentha Counter (e.g., ₹25,000)
                             │
                             ▼
     2. Cashier enters customer's mobile number in the POS Billing Counter
        (or enrolls a new walk-in customer in 30 seconds via OTP)
                             │
                             ▼
     3. POS Counter immediately displays:
        • Available HUBKLUB Balance (e.g., 1,250 pts = ₹1,250 discount)
        • GO SILVER (2027–2028) Celebration Strip & Milestone Status
                             │
                             ▼
     4. Customer redeems 1,000 points:
        • Gross Bill Amount: ₹25,000
        • Points Redeemed: -₹1,000 (1,000 pts)
        • Net Payable: ₹24,000
        • Points Earned Today (5% slab for ₹20k–₹50k): +1,200 points
                             │
                             ▼
     5. Cashier completes checkout with UPI / Card:
        • Digital store invoice is created with Tally voucher sync
        • Loyalty Statement and GO SILVER status are updated
                             │
                             ▼
     6. Customer's Mobile App updates in real time:
        • Digital bill saved in "My Orders & Store Bills"
        • Points wallet updated with new balance
        • GO SILVER tracker reflects qualifying celebration spend
```
